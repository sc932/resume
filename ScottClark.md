## Executive career summary (reverse-chronological)

### Distributional, Inc. — Co-founder & CEO (July 2023 – present)
Enterprise AI reliability company; pivoted to **Talaria Scientific** in July 2026, a multi-agent harness for computational science research ([the announcement](https://distributional.com/blog/distributional-is-now-talaria)). Palo Alto, CA HQ. Raised $30M across Seed (Dec 2023, a16z) and Series A (Oct 2024, Two Sigma Ventures). Team of 30 as of 2025. Product: test, calibrate, and monitor AI systems in production to surface behavioral issues from trace data. Customers include multiple Fortune 500 enterprises. US Patent 12,505,027 granted 2025 (anomaly detection in deployed AI applications, Scott as named inventor).

### Intel Corporation — VP & GM, AI and HPC Supercomputing (November 2020 – June 2023)
Based in Portland, Oregon. Joined through Intel's acquisition of SigOpt (announced 2020-10-29). Led a **~200-person multi-disciplinary organization** responsible for application-level AI and HPC software within Intel's Supercomputing Group. **Three successive promotions** across 2 years 7 months:

- **November 2020 – March 2021:** Director & GM of SigOpt (post-acquisition integration into Intel's Machine Learning Performance team within IAGS).
- **March 2021 – September 2022:** Senior Director & GM of SigOpt and AI Application Enablement (expanded scope beyond SigOpt into Intel Supercomputing Group's AI application enablement).
- **September 2022 – June 2023:** **VP & GM of AI and HPC Supercomputing Application Level Engineering** (terminal title). Led ~200 people in AI/HPC software for Intel's supercomputing customers.

Public face for Intel's oneAPI open-ecosystem strategy. Notable appearances: SC22 theCUBE panel (Dallas, November 2022, w/ David Schmidt of Dell); MLconf 2021 Webinar; Ai4 2021; Intel Conversations in the Cloud Episode 250 (July 2021). Integrated SigOpt's IP into Intel's AI Analytics Toolkit. Recognized as a leader in AI Software Optimization (Kisaco Research Leadership Council, March 2021).

### SigOpt, Inc. — Co-founder & CEO (November 2014 – November 2020)
Based in San Francisco, CA. Y Combinator W15 cohort. Commercial Bayesian-optimization platform serving Fortune 500 enterprises in algorithmic trading, intelligence, enterprise technology, and consumer tech. Acquired by Intel Corporation on 2020-10-29 (terms undisclosed; TechCrunch reported "less than $10M prior funding" reflecting publicly-known rounds only).

**Co-founders:** Patrick Hayes (CTO 2014–2021; now at Airtable), Eric Liu.

**Funding history (total ~$17M):**

- 2015-06-16: $2M seed led by **Andreessen Horowitz**, participants: Data Collective, Y Combinator (W15).
- 2016-08-24: $6.6M Series A led by **Andreessen Horowitz** (repeat), participants: Data Collective, SV Angel, Stanford University, Blumberg Capital.
- 2018 (unpublicized at close): **~$8.5M Series A+** led by **Blumberg Capital (Matt Bornstein)**, with **Two Sigma Investments** co-participating. Scott-disclosed in 2026-04 interview. Two Sigma's participation was publicly announced 2019-03-05 alongside a commercial partnership via PRNewswire + SigOpt company blog (Two Sigma also became customer + advisor).
- 2018-02: **In-Q-Tel** strategic investment (U.S. intelligence-community VC; amount undisclosed per IQT policy).

**Research and IP output (2014–2020):** 15 peer-reviewed papers on Bayesian optimization and hyperparameter tuning; ~20 granted US patents as named inventor. Major patent families: Intelligent Optimization Platform (11 patents), Multi-Criteria Optimization (7 patents), Multi-Solution Hyperparameter Tuning (2 patents + pending). Public recognition: Gartner Cool Vendor in AI Core Technologies (2017); Barclays Open Innovation Challenge winner (2017); Kisaco Research KLC Leader (2021).

**Notable SigOpt research leadership note:** Scott intentionally did not add himself as author on every SigOpt research paper, preferring to give junior team members or those heading back to academia the authorship boost. Author lists are a **lower bound** on his research involvement. He shaped research direction across the corpus.

### Yelp, Inc. — Software Engineer & Team Lead, Ad Targeting (July 2012 – December 2014)
Based in San Francisco, CA. Title on 2014-08-10 dated resume: "Data Mining Engineer and Lead on Ad Targeting Team." Per the Cornell ORIE alumni spotlight, Scott was **"one of the first Ph.D.'s hired by Yelp."**

- **Co-developed MOE** (Metric Optimization Engine, [github.com/Yelp/MOE](https://github.com/Yelp/MOE)) — the first production Bayesian-optimization open-source package. MOE became Yelp's third-most-popular open-source release within 72 hours of launch. Basis of SigOpt's founding.
- **Director, Yelp Dataset Challenge** (`yelp.com/dataset_challenge`) — created + implemented + directed; used by 100,000+ students worldwide since inception.
- **Founded Yelp's internal Applied Learning Group** — bi-weekly all-engineering technical speaker series.
- Implemented multi-armed bandit strategies for ad selection; sole targeting engineer on mobile app ads rollout.
- **Press:** *Wall Street Journal* profile, 2014-08-08 (Elizabeth Dwoskin, "Big Data's High-Priests of Algorithms / Academic Researchers Find Lucrative Work as 'Big Data' Scientists"). Scott quoted: *"Academia is slow and only a few people see your work. At Yelp, I can be pushing out experiments that affect hundreds of millions of people. When I make a small change to the Yelp website, I have a bigger impact."*

New York, NY. Built an end-to-end portfolio-analytics function in C++ and JavaScript during DOE CSGF summer practicum window.
